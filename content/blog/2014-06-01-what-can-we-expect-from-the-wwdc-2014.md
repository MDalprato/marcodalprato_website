“Write the code, change the world”.

This is what is written in the first floor of the Moscone Center (San Francisco, CA) where the 2014 WWDC will be held.

The developing of mobile apps has increase its potential and reliability in the last five years more then every other technology in the world of smartphones and tablets.

Apple have introduced a neat diversification between the O.S and the apps: the first must be closed, safe and without any kind of communication with the rest of the world the second, instead, must be open and ready to receive and send a lot quantity of data between the smartphone and the rest of the world.

Less then one year ago we have installed iOS7 that has completely changed our way to interact with our iPhones with a lot of new features: quick access for bluetooth, wifi ecc.., a new multitasking manager, new icons, and (for the first time ever) a mobile OS that was created for a 64 bit processor.

Nobody (except Tim Cook and his crew) known what will be introduced during the 2014 WWDC and what will be included in the new iOS8 but I’m sure that it will be something about health, physical activity and music ( Read more about the Beats acquisition ).

Maybe the old Macbook Pro (the one with the dvd drive) will be definitely deleted from the Apple’s Store and the current line of Macbook Pro Retina will be revisited with new processor (ARM?) and new graphics (the macbook pro 13 retina needs a better on board graphic card) and maybe with more GB of solid state memory (128 Gb, in my opinion are too few).

Perhaps the Macbook Air will receive a better display (wishing for one with a retina resolution) that will allow it to became the best, thinner and reliable laptop ever.

In this WWDC we could definitely see the new iOS8 and the new **OS X** that will reduce the difference between the mobile and the desktop O.S because, I think, the future is a world with a unique O.S. for both of the environment.

I really don’t know what we can expect from the WWDC 2014 but it will be the most awesome WWDC of ever!
