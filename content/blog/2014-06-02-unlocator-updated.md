I love watching Tv shows and movies but, unfortunately, in this country (Italy) there isn’t a reliable service for video streaming like **Netflix** and this is the reason why I use Unlocator.

Unlocator, in my opinion, is one of the best DNS service that allow you to watch **Netflix** and all the other service like Hulu, HBO ecc.. outside the USA.

Unfortunately here in Italy I can have only a dynamic IP and this is a really boring thing because I’ve to check every time if the IP that Unlocator has saved is the really one that I’ve in that particular moment.

Yesterday I’ve decided to solve this problem forever creating an app for OSX.

This app is really simple and really useful and it can auto-check your IP every hour 60/600/1800/3600/18000 seconds (Latest version is required) and sends your right IP to Unlocator (It’s something like NO-IP, dyndns …) First of all: it’s a BETA so it could be full of bugs … if you want send me an email with all the bugs that you’ll find I’ll try to keep it updated.
