I’m writing this post from the room of my hotel here in New York.

If there is a thing that I understand being here for less then a week is that New York is really the city that never sleeps and when I say never I really say NEVER.

There isn’t a free second or a free moment here: everything is changing every minutes and the word “progress” takes a different meaning here.

When I went off the cab the first day (on monday morning) I felt a little bit scared … too much skyscrapers, too much people and too much noise, but after a fresh shower and a fast nap I went back into the heart of the City and everything has taken another meaning.

One of the most important thing that I learn here in the City is that everybody walk too fast and there’s no respect for all the traffic lights in the streets: if is white you can go, if is blinking red you can go and … if is red you can go (just pay attention to the cabs).

It’s something that you can really feel in the air … the ideas begin and take shape in every seconds: it’s the CITY.

There are a lot of amazing things here that you can do: walking around without a reason, spend all your day in Central park, walk around Times Square or empty your wallet in one of the hundred shops that are on the Fifth.

This city it’s exactly like I imagined before the flight that brought me in NY from Frankfurt: full of people, full of light and full of energy.

The Energy is the most awesome thing that you can really feel when you walk along the street of the Big Apple: it’s a particular kind of energy that allow the growing of ideas here more than anything other places on the earth.

New York is the city where I want to live, work and die.

Every day there’s something different, something better and something new, it’s a daily run against the time because if you live in the City you are always full of things to do.

I’ve been in New York for less than a week and after that week I’m more than ever convinced to live in it.

There is something magic in that city and it’s really the city that never sleeps.
