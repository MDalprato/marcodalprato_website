Apple has always said that the **Apple TV** is an Hobby and nothing else but what happen if Apple decides to transform it in more than an “hobby”?

At the moment of writing the **Apple TV** has only a few apps ready “ out of the box ” like **Netflix**, ABC, HBO Go, **iTunes** Trailer etc. but if Apple decides to “open” the **Apple TV** and to create a new App Store only for the ATV?

It will be amazing: thousands of apps all in your TV like iMessage, Twitter, Facebook, Whatsapp etc. ready to be used with your remote control ( or with the remote App ).

The **Apple TV** will be a completely new kind of device with a personal store and a personal interaction with the external environment.

The **Apple TV** is a device that you connect to the television with an HDMI cable and that is ready to work as you get out it from the box (and the box is a really gorgeous box).

No configuration is needed except for the **Wi-Fi** settings and the **iTunes** ID but all the apps are already installed and there’s no way to delete or add something.

Just think if you can add your favorite app like Twitter or Facebook and receive a notify when you are watching a movie or a TV show: it would be pretty great.

The potential of the **Apple TV**, as I said, could be huge and if Apple decide to create a “ATV store” with a lot of Apps that you can add to the **Apple TV** I think that a new kind of revolution will start.

Just think about the new featured called “HomeKit” that will be presented in iOS 8 and think if something like that could take place inside the **Apple TV**: manage the Hue Lights, the Belkin Wemo switches or keep an eye on your domotic system would be some of the incredible things that you could do with your **Apple TV**.

FaceTime and Skype could be upgraded to use the **Apple TV** as a second monitor; Spotify could create an app for the ATV so finally you could listening to your favorite music directly from the TV.

The implementation of the App Store on the **Apple TV** will be a huge opportunities for Apple because it will be the perfect extension of the iOS ecosystem: **iPhone**, **iPad** & **Apple TV**.

The ATV could really become a parallel device like the **iPhone**: with a bigger internal memory and a powerful hardware, Apple could think to the world of gamers.

There will be no mercy for the other companies: Apple will come strongly in the market of game consoles kicking off the Playstation and the Xbox in less then three years.

Games with a price of 6$ purchased directly from the **Apple TV** Store without any stupid Blu-ray or physical disk: and if you buy the **Apple TV** version you receive the corresponding version for iOS (**iPhone** and **iPad**).

There’s a huge market that are waiting for a revolution like that because people are bored of slow and stupid configurations or ugly devices connected to the television: people wants an unique device that can do everything.

If you are a developer, indeed, you could finally create all the apps that you want directly from your Mac using Xcode (Apple will give an **Apple TV** simulator) and it will provide new APIs that will allow the communication between the **Apple TV** and the iOS devices.

A unique device with 32GB of memory (for apps, movies and rents) and a A7/A8 processor with a dedicate graphics unit and (maybe) the support for 4k televisions it’s what I can call “ The next big thing “.

Imagine that Tim Cook takes the stage of the Moscone Center and says:: Sorry guys … we have another big thing and it’s called the **Apple TV** … with Apps!

Changing the rules of what is the television is the new business that Reed Hastings has already understood with his creation called “**Netflix**”.

The television needs a revolution that should change everything from the bottom to the top and Apple should do something right now.

The **Apple TV** is like a heaven garden with a golden door that can’t be open without the Apple authorization and this is very boring because in that garden there’s a lot of fun things but nobody is allowed to change anything.

A lot of amazing things could be done if only Apple decides to create an **Apple TV** App Store, it would be really the next big thing and the impact in our life would be great!.

Stay Tuned.
