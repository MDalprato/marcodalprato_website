I love the world of the watches.

Everything started six years ago when I saw for the first time “007 Casino Royale” and I heard the discussion between Bond and Vesper when she asked to him what brand of watches he wore: it was an Omega.

For who, like me, is a fan of expensive watches, that Omega was a Seamaster Planet Ocean 2807.50.91 but for all the other people was “something like a Rolex”.

I wore a Casio G-shock when I was a teenager because it was cheaper and everyone had one on their wrists (when you are a teenager you don’t follow your taste, you follow the herd) and after the “Ugly-Tennager-Casio” I owned a classic quartz watch (I’m pretty sure that it was a Sector but I can’t remember the model).

At the end of the day I’m not a watch-guy because I don’t like cheap watches: they aren’t unique, priceless or made at the state of the art; they are something that everyone can buy and wear without having 10000 $ in they bank account.

In the 2010 I bought the **iPhone** 4 and I started to leave the watch on the desk of my bedside table and after a month I put it in a box under the bed and I forgot it in there.

Don’t get me wrong, as I said before I love watches, but only the ones that I won’t buy (and I can’t) like Omega, Patek Philippe, Rolex and A lange and Sohne that is my favorite at all.

The Saxonia Automatic Outsized Date is not a watch, is a piece of art that you can wear on you wrist (after that you payed 50.000 $) and feel like one of the few people in the world that could really afford and own that watch.

When you buy a watch like that, you buy a piece of art and technology that could be compared to a supercar or a boeing 777 inside a little and shiny box: isn’t a “simple” watch.

I was really tempted to buy a used Omega Speedmaster Moonwatch Professional 42 mm (311.30.42.30.01.005): I searched online, I visited a lot of physical stores and I tried different models that could fit my wrist but at the end of the search I didn’t buy anything.

I always loved the mechanics, the steel and the technology that is behind a classic watch but I’m a tech-nerd-geek guy that loves the electron and the silicon and this “dark side of me” is not attracted to the classic watch … it deserve a smart-watch.

Seriously, I know that my mind is strange but for some reason I can’t buy a classic watch.

I love them but I can’t buy it and that’s all.

The **Apple Watch** is ready to be sold and in less then a few months we will see it on the desks of the Apple stores, so I said to my VISA (believe me or not but I often speak with my credit card): I’m going to wait the **Apple Watch** and if isn’t cool I won’t buy it …. otherwise I’ll do.

I also tried to say to myself: don’t be stupid, you don’t need another useless gadget because my house is full of this kind of things but I didn’t hear, I couldn’t.

I’m enough sure that I’m going to buy the Apple watch (the one with the dark stainless case) because I love that kind of geek-things … I already feel like James Bond in one of his great adventures: the **Apple Watch** isn’t a watch but an extension of the **iPhone** and for this reason it’ll become useful as your phone charger.

The future of the watch is here and, believe me or not, there’s nothing you can do to stop it.

We can compare the “war” between the classic watch and the smart watch like the “war” between the vinyl and the digital audio: everyone desire and dream a great turntable and a terrific vinyl collection but at the end of the day we have already downloaded giga and giga of music from **iTunes** or Spotify.

The idea behind the “smart watch” (and in this particular case the **Apple Watch**) is the extension of something that everyone is using (the Smartphone) and something that a lot of person doesn’t use anymore (the watch).

Entering in the world of the watches is a big deal that (in my opinion) could really change some things like the quartz movement made a few of decades ago and I think that now is the right time for a change in that direction.

We need someone that want to change the rules of the game and we need new technologies that can improve the world of the watches and I think that we will see something really awesome when the Apple watch will be released this March (this is the rumor that shakes the web right now).

The only thing that we can do is to wait.

Stay Tuned.

I’ll be back soon.
