09/10/2014 - The best TV Series that you should watch now.

It’s fall so it’s foggy and cold outside:  this is the perfect time of the year to watch some new TV Series or to waste all your free time on Netflix so I wrote a list of the best TV Series that are on air right now.
Forever
ABC Channel (Tuesdays 10|9c)
Forever is a new TV Series made by ABC and sets in New York
(
What a wonderful city
)
that talks about a man, Henry Morgan, who can live
forever
.
It’s a cool and fresh TV Series that match a lot of different thing all together. Suggested if you like something like CSI but without all the dark and weird situations of Grissom & Co.
Gotham
Fox TV (Friday 9|8c)
This Tv Series doesn’t need any explanation: it’s the best TV Series on the market in this moment.
It’s the story of Gotham (New York is everywhere ….) during the childhood of Bruce wayne and tells about all the criminals that live in the dark city.
It’s really beautiful and Ben McKenzie is a pleasant discovery as Detective Gordon.
Selfie
ABC Channel (Tuesdays 8|7c)
Silly but relaxing : Selfie talks about a girl (Eliza) who passes her entire life “in the Cloud” jumping from Instagram to Facebook but one day she decide to change her life and to became more “human”.
Watch the pilot from the ABC website and you’ll not be disappointed.
