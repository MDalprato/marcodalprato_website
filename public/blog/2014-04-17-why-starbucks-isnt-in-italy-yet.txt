17/04/2014 - Why Starbucks isn’t in Italy yet ?

First of all :
I’m an addicted to American Coffee.
I need a full cup of hot coffee every day of my life otherwise I can’t do nothing: it’s like a V8 engine without gasoline in the tank.
Unfortunately here in Italy, in this useless land, it’s impossibile to find a public place where you can buy a great cup of Coffee.
In the perimeter of my little (little) town I can only drink a miserable substitute of the original American Coffee in the nearest McDonald (in Faenza) and, because that reason, the only way to enjoy a cup of coffee is the “self-made-way”.
So one year ago I bought a Philips coffee maker that allow me to enjoy the real American Coffee every morning/evening or when I want it.
I don’t know the exactly reason, maybe Italian people are too much proud of their own Italian Coffee or their habits are too much deep into the Italian history of Coffee, but they can’t even considerate the possibility to drink an amazing-tasting American Coffee.
There are strange ideas about the American Coffee: Italian people think that it’s strange, not normal or too much “Americano” but in my opinion the American Coffee is the only way to spend some good time with theme self (or, sometimes, to share a moment with someone else ).
On may 2014 I’ll go to New York city for one week and, after all the amazing things that I will see, there is one that I want to do : drink a great American Coffee (in one of the many Starbucks that are in NYC).
Still 31 days.
Stay Tuned.
Marco.
