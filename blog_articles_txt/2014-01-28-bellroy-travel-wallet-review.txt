28/01/2014 - Bellroy Travel Wallet Review

Preface
This is my first post write in english so it’s possible that I may have made some mistakes but as someone said : if you never try … you will never learn.
This review it’s all about my new
“last week” purchase : the Bellroy Travel Wallet.
This wallet it’s different between the other ones : it’s bigger enough to contain a Travel Passport (tested by me with an Italian one), can contains up to seven Credit Cards, a mobile sim card pocket and a micro mobile pen (very useful if you are on a plane and you need to compile some documents).
I ordered the wallet from the official Bellroy website (
here
) on the 3th of January and i choose the express shipment.
The wallet arrived to my home on the 16th of January (you can choose two option : free shipment that is, of course, free or express shipment that (here in Italy) cost 10 €.
The packaging is really amazing : it’s all made of recyclable paper and it’s sewn to the edged; it’s look very refined and, in this case, contains a spare ink cartridge (it’s a common “pilot” ink cartridge that you can find on Amazon).
After a first look to the wallet i realize that it’s made with a lot of attention and all the parts have been studied down to the smallest details.
The first thing that i notice it’s the awesome smell of true leather and all the part looks really solid.
Inside the “box” there’s a “false” passport with some tips about the most important thing that you can do when you start a trip (how to reduce the thickness of your wallet, how to organize your suitcase etc …).
As you can see in the upper picture, my passport fit very well inside the wallet and there’s no problem to carry it together with all the credit card and the money.
The most important part of this wallet is the bill container where all kind of banknotes can fit very well (i’ve tried with a piece of 100€ and a piece of 100$ and there no problems to put it together).
As you can see in the picture above, inside the wallet there are space for three credit cards (in the picture i used my Lufthansa Miles&More Card).
The quality is good and the price is right (105€) so i recommend this wallet for everyone who are in planning to make a trip.
Stay Tuned.
